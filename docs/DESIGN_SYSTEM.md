# Blob Fit — Design system

## Canonical mascot
Warm cream-white rounded/amorphous soft-clay figure; smooth pill-like black eyes only (no nose, hair, mouth, ears); **black athletic shorts ALWAYS**. The app's Blob is at a slightly stronger/confident stage of the story without human anatomy, exaggerated muscles or a new face. Supporting colored characters, if introduced later, are not the protagonist.

## Visual direction
Editorial, calm, premium, warm natural light, off-white/cream backgrounds, nearly-black typography, soft gray surfaces, restrained peach/orange progress accent. Content-heavy screens can use colored accents to differentiate types, not copy the competitor's bright-green graphic system.

## Suggested initial tokens — implementation defaults, not color-exact samples
Background #F8F5EF; surface #FFFFFF; ink #18191B; secondary #74716F; accent #F58C62; border #E9E4DE; success #528C66; danger #D85550. 12–20pt corner radii; 8pt spacing scale; strong headings; clear 44pt minimum touch areas.

## Navigation
Home, Plan, Progress, More. Onboarding first run; exercise detail from plan and library; workouts have a persistent timer and log sets.

## Visual references
Approved FIVE mobile screen concepts live under assets/app-screens when installed. They are reference images, NOT full-screen app UI. Use their hierarchy and aesthetic, but recreate all elements as interactive native components. Do not trust any placeholder figures or misleading exercise variation labels.

## Illustration QA
Blob wears shorts in all images, including anatomy diagrams and exercise cards. Keep consistency in body shape, eyes, material. Different training poses and camera angles; cover must show the movement being explained.
