# Exercise art brief

Generated by `tools/art/exercise-brief.mjs` from `data/exercises.json`. Do not edit by hand. **31 of 257 images delivered.**

This is a request for production art, not a claim that it exists. The app must not ship with missing exercise art: `npm run release:check` fails until every file below is delivered.

## What to draw

For every exercise:
- **`start.png` and `end.png`:** the two key positions of a correct rep, from the camera view given. The app loops them to show the movement, and they teach most of it: the screen has almost no text.
- **`mistake-<n>.png`:** one image per common mistake, showing that fault clearly but realistically, from the view given. The app puts it next to a one-line fix, so the image itself has no red cross, arrows or text.

**One sheet per exercise.** All missing frames of an exercise are drawn in one image, in rows of up to three, read left to right, top row first: start and end (always together), then the missing mistakes in order. Drawing them together keeps the character, camera and props the same, so the start → end loop does not jump. `npm run art:import` cuts the sheet into the files listed below.

**Ready-to-paste prompts:** one per exercise, in [`docs/art-prompts/`](art-prompts/). Sheets still needed: batch 1 2, batch 2 25, batch 3 19 (missing images: 4 / 126 / 96).

## Spec (same for every file)
- **Character:**
  - The canonical Blob: warm cream-white soft clay, two upright black pill eyes, no mouth, nose, hair or ears.
  - **Black athletic shorts**, beginner build (S0–S1), same proportions in every frame.
  - No extra muscles, no recolors. Use the founder handoff `base-character-sheet.png` as the reference for every image.
- **Canvas:**
  - One landscape PNG per exercise, at least 1536 × 1024 px (larger is better), with a transparent background (RGBA).
  - Exactly the figures the prompt lists, in rows of up to three, with empty space around each; nothing touches the image edge or another figure.
  - No grid lines, floor, room, scenery, text, logo, arrows or ticks. A faint contact shadow under the body is fine.
- **Camera and scale:**
  - Camera at hip height, using the view listed for that figure.
  - Same character size in every figure. Pixel-exact alignment is not needed: `npm run art:sync` puts every frame of an exercise on one shared scale and the same ground point.
- **Props:** simple, neutral and realistic: dark grey dumbbells, a muted band with its anchor visible, a plain bench, chair or wall. No brand marks.
- **Technique:** the correct frames must match the text, because a coach reviews both. When the text and a drawing disagree, the text wins; flag it in issue #1.

## Delivery
- **File name:** the exercise id, for example `hip-hinge.png`. The id is in each prompt heading.
- **Send it, one of:**
  - Paste the sheets into the Claude Code chat. Claude saves them under the right name.
  - Or upload them to `assets/exercises/inbox/` on branch `claude/zealous-cannon-9cvkob` (GitHub: Add file → Upload files).
- **After delivery:** Claude runs `npm run art:import`. It cuts each sheet into the files below, then `art:sync` validates them, aligns the frames, writes optimized WebP copies and wires them into the app. Claude checks every figure against the exercise text and answers with one list of the frames to redo, if any; rejected frames come back in the next prompt.
- **Order:** batch 1 (the beginner plan) first, then batch 2 (their variations and the bodyweight movements), then batch 3.

- [ ] `assets/exercises/dumbbell-floor-press/start.png`: Top: Lying on the back on a mat, knees bent and feet flat, arms straight holding two dumbbells above the chest, palms facing the feet, wrists straight.
- [ ] `assets/exercises/dumbbell-floor-press/end.png`: Bottom: Same lying position, elbows bent about 45° from the torso, upper arms resting lightly on the mat, forearms vertical, each dumbbell straight above its elbow, wrists straight.
- [ ] `assets/exercises/dumbbell-floor-press/mistake-1.png`: Wrong on purpose (from above): **Elbows flaring into a T.** Your elbows land on the floor straight out from your shoulders, near 90° from your torso, like the letter T.
- [ ] `assets/exercises/dumbbell-floor-press/mistake-2.png`: Wrong on purpose (side view): **Hips lifting off the floor.** As you press, your hips lift off the floor and your back arches, so your body forms a bridge.
- [ ] `assets/exercises/dumbbell-floor-press/mistake-3.png`: Wrong on purpose (side view): **Wrists bent back.** Your wrists fold back so the dumbbells sit behind your forearms, knuckles pointing toward your head instead of the ceiling.

### Dumbbell Shoulder Press (`dumbbell-shoulder-press`), side view

Equipment: two dumbbells and a chair with a back. Setup: Sit tall on a sturdy chair, back against the backrest, feet flat and hip-width apart. Dumbbells at shoulder height, palms forward or slightly in, forearms vertical. Ribs down, eyes forward.

- [ ] `assets/exercises/dumbbell-shoulder-press/start.png`: Start: Seated tall on a chair, back against the backrest, feet flat hip-width, dumbbells at shoulder height, palms forward, forearms vertical, elbows slightly in front of the torso.
- [ ] `assets/exercises/dumbbell-shoulder-press/end.png`: Top: Same seated position, arms straight overhead, dumbbells over the shoulders, arms beside the ears, ribs down, lower back still against the backrest, feet flat.
- [ ] `assets/exercises/dumbbell-shoulder-press/mistake-1.png`: Wrong on purpose (side view): **Arching the lower back.** As the dumbbells go up, your ribs flare, your lower back peels off the chair, and your chest tilts toward the ceiling.
- [ ] `assets/exercises/dumbbell-shoulder-press/mistake-2.png`: Wrong on purpose (side view): **Pressing out in front.** At the top, the dumbbells finish in front of your face, with your arms angled forward instead of beside your ears.
- [ ] `assets/exercises/dumbbell-shoulder-press/mistake-3.png`: Wrong on purpose (front view): **Forearms tilting out of line.** From the front, your elbows flare out wide or pinch in, so the dumbbells sit outside or inside your elbows instead of above them.

### Band Chest Press (`band-chest-press`), side view

- [ ] `assets/exercises/band-chest-press/mistake-3.png`: Wrong on purpose (side view): **Letting the band snap back.** Your hands fly back past your chest and your elbows shoot well behind your body as the band pulls.

### Band Pull-Apart (`band-pull-apart`), front view

Equipment: light resistance band. Setup: Stand tall, feet hip-width apart, knees soft. Hold the band with both hands, palms down, about shoulder-width apart. Arms straight but not locked at chest height, band slightly taut. Shoulders down, eyes forward.

- [ ] `assets/exercises/band-pull-apart/start.png`: Start: Standing tall, feet hip-width apart, knees soft. Arms straight forward at chest height, hands shoulder-width apart, palms down, holding a light band with slight tension.

- [ ] `assets/exercises/band-pull-apart/mistake-1.png`: Wrong on purpose (front view): **Shrugging toward the ears.** As the band stretches, your shoulders rise and the space between your shoulders and ears shrinks, most of all at the end of the pull.
- [ ] `assets/exercises/band-pull-apart/mistake-2.png`: Wrong on purpose (side view): **Arching the lower back.** At the end of the pull, your ribs flare, your chest pushes forward and your lower back arches as you lean back.
- [ ] `assets/exercises/band-pull-apart/mistake-3.png`: Wrong on purpose (front view): **Bending the elbows.** Your elbows bend to 90° or more as you pull, and your hands end near your shoulders, more like a row than a pull-apart.

### Band Face Pull (`band-face-pull`), side view

- [ ] `assets/exercises/band-face-pull/mistake-1.png`: Wrong on purpose (side view): **Elbows dropping into a row.** Your elbows sink below shoulder height and stay near your sides, so the band ends at your chest or chin, not your eyes.

- [ ] `assets/exercises/band-face-pull/mistake-3.png`: Wrong on purpose (side view): **Leaning back to pull.** Your torso tips back 15° or more as you pull, and your hips push forward to help bring the band toward you.

### Dumbbell Bent-Over Row (`dumbbell-bent-over-row`), side view

Equipment: two dumbbells. Setup: Feet hip-width apart, knees soft, hinged at the hips so your torso is about 30–45° above horizontal. Back flat, neck in line, eyes on the floor a few feet ahead. Dumbbells hang under your shoulders, palms facing in.

- [ ] `assets/exercises/dumbbell-bent-over-row/mistake-1.png`: Wrong on purpose (side view): **Standing up to finish the rep.** Your torso rises from about 45° toward upright as you pull, so the dumbbells finish near your stomach with your chest lifted.
- [ ] `assets/exercises/dumbbell-bent-over-row/mistake-2.png`: Wrong on purpose (side view): **Rounding the back.** Your back curves into a C from hips to head, your head drops and the dumbbells drift forward in front of your knees.
- [ ] `assets/exercises/dumbbell-bent-over-row/mistake-3.png`: Wrong on purpose (front view): **Pulling toward the chest.** Your elbows flare out wide, the dumbbells travel straight up toward your chest and your shoulders rise toward your ears.

- [ ] `assets/exercises/band-lat-pulldown/mistake-2.png`: Wrong on purpose (back view): **Shoulders stuck up by the ears.** Your shoulders stay hunched up through the pull, and your elbows stop around shoulder height instead of reaching your ribs.
- [ ] `assets/exercises/band-lat-pulldown/mistake-3.png`: Wrong on purpose (side view): **Crunching the chest down.** Your chest drops and your upper back rounds as you pull, like a crunch, and your elbows finish in front of your body.

### Pallof Press (`pallof-press`), front view

Equipment: resistance band anchored at chest height, to your side. Setup: Band anchored at chest height. Stand side-on to the anchor, a step or two away so the band is taut. Feet hip- to shoulder-width apart, knees soft. Both hands hold the band at the middle of your chest. Eyes straight ahead.

- [ ] `assets/exercises/pallof-press/mistake-3.png`: Wrong on purpose (side view): **Arching your lower back.** As your arms go out, your ribs lift, your lower back arches and your hips push forward.
