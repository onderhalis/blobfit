## Character lock
Same warm cream-white soft-clay Blob as canonical references, two upright black pill eyes, no mouth/nose/hair/ears, **black athletic shorts on every pose**. Do not change the character's proportions, add cartoon muscles, or swap in a colored supporting character. Keep pose readable on an off-white app surface; avoid shadows painted as an opaque rectangular background. Natural soft 3D volume and premium editorial lighting. All exports should have a genuinely transparent alpha channel.

## QA
Check the silhouette, eye placement, shorts visibility, feet/hands and props, no extra limbs, no cut-off body parts. Exercise poses are illustrative and require professional technique review before publication. Do not repurpose exercise illustration as personalized instruction without context.
