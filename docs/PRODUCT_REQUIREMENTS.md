# Blob Fit — MVP requirements

## Purpose
An English-first iOS/Android fitness app with a single original Blob mascot, an approachable coach who has learned how to train. The user's progress is real, not the mascot's invented stats.

## MVP flows
1. Onboarding: choose goal (build muscle, lose fat, improve posture, get stronger), experience, workout days per week, available equipment, consent to general fitness disclaimer.
2. Home: show actual next workout, current streak only from completed sessions, Resume or Start button, navigate to Plan, Library, Progress.
3. Plan: beginner full-body A/B rotation (squat, incline push-up, supported dumbbell row, hip hinge, plank) adaptable to equipment and capability. Rest days are legitimate.
4. Exercise detail: muscles trained, starting position, eccentric/concentric phases, form cues, mistakes, easier/harder versions. Display qualified-review status for illustrative anatomy and training guidance.
5. Workout: start/pause/complete session; log sets/reps/load/duration, skip or substitute exercise; locally persist data.
6. Progress: adherence, sessions, volume and personal records calculated only from logs; empty state for brand-new users.

## Non-goals for v1
Subscriptions, chat-based AI trainer, medical/rehabilitation diagnosis, body photo analysis, accounts/cloud sync, calorie predictions, fabricated physical transformation, push-notification growth loops.

## Acceptance criteria
All five screens functional and responsive; plan can be completed offline; progress updates after logging; no fake metrics; black shorts on every Blob depiction; English copy; usable accessibility labels; transparent build/test status.

## Key product differentiation
Character-led encouragement meets credible beginner instruction. Social series and app share brand identity but app exercise prescriptions are reviewed independently of marketing visuals.
